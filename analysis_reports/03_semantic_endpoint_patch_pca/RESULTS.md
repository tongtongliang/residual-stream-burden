# Semantic-Endpoint Patch PCA Results

## Protocol

- Models: DeCO-B, PixelDiT-B, and DiP-B velocity.
- Checkpoints: raw epoch-200 states, step 250200.
- Endpoint: `silu(t_embedding + semantic_block_output)` immediately before
  the separate pixel decoder. Decoder activations are excluded.
- Data: the registered balanced ImageNet-train 100k selection, including the
  same deterministic horizontal flips used by the raw-patch PCA.
- Conditioning: true class.
- Noise: one deterministic Gaussian realization per image, shared across all
  nine values `t=0.1,...,0.9` and all three models.
- Observations: 25.6M endpoint patch vectors per model and `t`.

All three model audits pass and share selection hash
`1ff3e31c1a6d79ed0399d8b7eb157fca7e5739cec6aba3c5096ad6cd653f4f7f`.

## Findings

1. At `t=0.5`, raw noisy RGB patches have effective rank `367.37`. The
   semantic endpoints are much narrower: DeCO-B `30.15`, PixelDiT-B `40.15`,
   and DiP-B `63.93`.
2. DeCO-B remains near an effective-rank plateau of approximately `30` from
   `t=0.4` through `t=0.9`. Its endpoint geometry is the most invariant to the
   input clean fraction in this range.
3. PixelDiT-B is non-monotonic: its effective rank peaks near `40.15` at
   `t=0.5` and contracts to `16.25` at `t=0.9`.
4. DiP-B is qualitatively different. Its endpoint effective rank rises from
   `8.21` at `t=0.1` to `159.00` at `t=0.9`, with `r90` increasing from `10`
   to `309`. Decoupling therefore does not universally imply a low-rank
   semantic endpoint.
5. Absolute covariance traces differ by orders of magnitude across models.
   They are reported because they are part of the measured geometry, but they
   reflect model-specific feature scaling and should primarily be compared
   across `t` within one model.

The result supports a narrower claim: all three decoupled models compress the
very high-dimensional mixed input at intermediate noise, but their semantic
interfaces allocate that bandwidth differently. DeCO maintains a compact
interface, PixelDiT contracts again toward clean inputs, and DiP expands its
semantic interface strongly as the clean signal increases.
